# Demo video script (T5)

30 seconds or less, screen recording of the live site with a voice-over. The guide makes anything longer ineligible for Global Judging, so export at 29 seconds. About 73 spoken words, read at a calm pace.

The beats follow the guide's own outline: who we are, the challenge, the project, how it works with NASA data, and what comes next.

Live site: https://mehdi-abidi.github.io/flame-in-freefall/

## Shots

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | Overview, top of the page, night mode. Caption: team name, "Flame in Freefall challenge". | "We are team [name], and this is Flame in Freefall." |
| 3 to 9 s | Hold on the headline. Click "On Earth" then "In orbit" under the flame. | "A lunar base will breathe 30% oxygen. NASA's solid-material fire tests in orbit stopped at 22.2%." |
| 9 to 15 s | Cabin lab. Click ISS (it reads Flown), then Lunar base (Never flown). | "Set your own cabin and see what NASA has burned there, and what it never has." |
| 15 to 20 s | Rank page. Click Lunar base, drag the Suppression slider up, let the list re-order. | "All 24 investigations re-rank live for your habitat." |
| 20 to 25 s | Ask page. Click the example "Which diluent put out droplet flames at lower oxygen, CO2 or helium?" Hover a citation. | "Ask a question. Every answer cites its NASA record." |
| 25 to 29 s | Overview, "What to test next". URL visible in the address bar. | "And it names the four experiments to fly next, before crews live on the Moon." |

## Rules from the 2026 guide

- English only. If any speech is in another language, add English subtitles.
- No NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers, and no music you do not have rights to.
- Nobody under 18 on screen or in the audio.
- Anything SI-generated in a video needs a visible watermark. A real screen recording with your own voice needs none, so keep it that way.
- The link must open with no sign-in. Test it in a private window.

## Before recording

1. Open the Ask page once and ask any question, then wait for the answer. The API sleeps when idle and the first answer can take up to a minute. After that, answers come in a few seconds. Ask the example question once too, so the recorded take is fast.
2. Use night mode (the default), browser zoom at 100%, window at 1920 x 1080, and close other tabs and notifications. Leave the ambient score off so it does not fight the voice.
3. Pre-load the Rank page so the switch is instant.

## Recording on a Mac

- Press Cmd + Shift + 5, choose Record Selected Portion, and under Options pick your microphone. Stop from the menu bar. The file lands on the Desktop.
- Trim in QuickTime with Cmd + T, or cut in iMovie if the voice is recorded separately.
- Export as MP4 or MOV, 1080p.

## Recording on Windows

- Press Win + Alt + R to start and stop recording with the Xbox Game Bar. The file goes to Videos > Captures.
- Record the voice-over in the same take, or record the screen silently and add the voice in any editor (Clipchamp is built into Windows).
- Export as MP4, 1080p.

## Upload

Upload to YouTube as Unlisted, or to Google Drive with "anyone with the link can view". Put the link in the submission form and in `submission-text.md`.
